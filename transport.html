How to Get There from Tokyo?

Ginzan Onsen is located on the inland side of Yamagata Prefecture and is about one hour away from Yamagata City. You can reach there by bullet train and bus, car or taxi

The Three Modes of Transportation

1. By Bullet Train (Shinkansen)


Take JR Yamagata Shinkansen to Oishida Station
schedule 3 hours 20 mins attach_money 12,500 yen one way

-> then take Hanagasa Bus that departs roughly every 60 - 90 mins
schedule 35 minutes attach_money 720 yen one way

-> for bus schedule Click here

2. By Rental Car

Rental cars are available at stations along the Yamagata Shinkansen such as Murayama, Shinjo and Yamagata stations, as well as at Yamagata Airport.

Parking lots:
- Ginzanonsen Kyodo Parking Lot Click here
schedule 5-10 minute walk to Ginzan Onsen (Closed from December – April)
attach_money ¥500 yen

- Taisho Romankan (Free parking, take a 5-10 minute ¥300 round-trip shuttle bus from here to Ginzan Onsen.) Click here

3. By Taxi


Start from 22,000 yen round-trip.

Note: Rental car can be convenient for exploring the area, however it is not recommended to drive during winter as the ground could be slippery from the snow.
